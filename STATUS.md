## Evidence and provenance boundaries

- Audit artifacts preserve run-level inputs and outputs, derived metrics and
  provenance. Multi-stage SUT records contain the final user-facing response
  and aggregate usage, not a transcript of every internal provider call.
- Tool-metered cost is preserved in metrics. Provider dashboards are the
  source of truth for pipeline stages and retries outside that meter; dashboard
  records are not themselves included in this repository.
- One audited upstream's prompts are not redistributed because no licence was
  provided; they are fetched from a pinned commit and hash-verified at runtime.
