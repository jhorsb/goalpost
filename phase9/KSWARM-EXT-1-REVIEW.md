## A) Novelty verdict table

| # | Claim (as written in PAPER.md) | Verdict | Decisive evidence (URL + verbatim quote) |
|---|---|---|---|
| (a) | "The IJCAI survey of robust counterfactual explanations [Jiang et al. 2024] taxonomises the field by *cause of change*: model update, input perturbation, noisy human execution. Re-query variance at identical inputs — no cause, no change — is absent from that taxonomy." | **SURVIVES (narrowly)** | The survey (https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01928; IJCAI 2024 proceedings paper 0894) states verbatim: "We found that the type of robustness falls within four separately studied categories, namely robustness against Model Changes (MC), Model Multiplicity (MM), Noisy Execution (NE), and Input Changes (IC)." Full-text search of both arXiv v1 and the IJCAI proceedings version: **zero hits** for stochastic/non-deterministic/identical/same-input/repeat/re-query/LLM/temperature/seed as an axis. No re-query axis exists → the omission claim is TRUE. Two narrowers: (1) the paper lists 3 of the survey's 4 categories (omits MM — see B6); (2) inside the survey's own MC lineage, run-to-run variance at the *parameter* level is already instrumented — Black, Wang & Fredrikson (ICLR 2022, https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.03109): "Random Seed (RS): Network parameters are initialized by incrementing the random seed across runs, while other hyperparameters and the data remain fixed." That is seed-variance across retrained models, not identical-model re-query variance, so the claim survives — but the gap is thinner than stated. |
| (b) | "Likewise the explanation-robustness literature [Alvarez-Melis & Jaakkola 2018] measures attribution stability across *neighbouring* inputs; identical-input free-text explanation is outside its instruments." | **WEAKENED** (survives only the narrowest reading) | AMJ itself is correctly characterized (https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.08049): "We argue that robustness of explanations—i.e., that similar inputs should give rise to similar explanations—is a key desideratum"; "All these methods return attribution arrays"; the local-Lipschitz instrument is degenerate at identical inputs, and its direct lineage (Yeh 2019, Fel 2022, Agarwal 2022) only perturbs inputs/models. BUT the wider literature falsifies any broad reading of "outside its instruments": (i) identical-input run-stability is a named, instrumented axis — S-LIME (https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.11757): "LIME is run 100 times on a randomly selected test point … If repeated runs using the same explanation algorithm on the same model to interpret the same data point yield different results, the utility of the explanation is brought into question"; DLIME (https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.10263): "LIME is producing different explanations for the same test instance… explanations which are generated with DLIME are deterministic and stable"; "Shaping Up SHAP" (https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.12115) defines stability as "deliver the same results under the same conditions across multiple executions". (ii) Identical-input **free-text** explanation stability has published instruments — Tanneru, Agarwal & Lakkaraju (AISTATS 2024, https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.03533): "we make one of the first attempts at quantifying the uncertainty in explanations of LLMs… Verbalized Uncertainty and Probing Uncertainty… probing uncertainty leverages sample and model perturbations"; LExT (FAccT 2025, https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.06227): "Iterative Stability: … The model is prompted five times, and the variance in cosine similarity between the explanations and the ground truth is calculated." Both **FALSIFY** the broad reading; the claim survives only scoped to AMJ's specific instruments/lineage. |
| (c) | "…re-query stability of decision-attached, LLM-authored recourse is an axis absent from the robustness literature's own taxonomies" AND "no prior work measures re-query stability of decision-attached, LLM-authored recourse, and none applies an LLM system's own advice back to the system that issued it." | Taxonomy-absence sentence: **SURVIVES (narrowly)**. Stronger compound sentence: **FALSIFIED in clause (ii)**, clause (i) **WEAKENED** | No recourse-robustness taxonomy contains the axis (canon checked: Mishra et al. https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.00358; Rawal/Upadhyay/Pawelczyk/De Toni/Dominguez-Olmedo line) → first sentence stands. Clause (ii) ("none applies an LLM system's own advice back to the system that issued it") is falsified by an entire self-consistency literature: Madsen, Chandar & Reddy (ACL Findings 2024, https://aclanthology.org/2024.findings-acl.19/): "we directly asked the language model to modify the resume such that it would yield a positive hiring recommendation"; "we can re-evaluate the prediction with the counterfactual and check that this goal is satisfied… a 'self-consistency check'" — re-evaluated on the issuing LLM in a new chat session, on a *hiring* decision. Mayne et al. (EMNLP 2025, https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1231.pdf): "Whether an SCE flips the LLM's prediction can be tested by re-evaluating the LLM in a new context window" — tabular high-stakes tasks, 50-variant decision-boundary consistency. Randl et al. (Machine Learning 2025, https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10994-025-06838-6): "a formula to estimate the number of tries needed to generate valid explanations" — validity checked on the issuing LLM. Entity-resolution paper (2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01210): "we apply the changes prescribed by the counterfactual explanations to the corresponding record pairs and re-query the LLM." Also falsified in agentic settings (Self-Refine, https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17651). Clause (i) is weakened: one-shot validity-upon-re-evaluation and prompt-variant consistency ARE measured above; what no work measures is *identical-case test-retest* stability of LLM-authored recourse specifically. Note also a neighboring literature has named the axis for judges — "post-decision robustness should be treated as a separate dimension of LLM-as-judge evaluation" (https://arxiv.org/html/2606.05384v1). Dong et al. 2026 distinction verified accurate (see B4). |
| (d) | "no stability claim is certified unless that reader clears a pre-registered self-agreement bar (with a stricter margin for claims of instability). Withheld is a first-class, publishable outcome." — `certified(s, a) ⇔ a ≥ 0.90 ∧ ( s ≥ 0.85 ∨ a − s ≥ 0.15 )` | **WEAKENED** (not falsified) | No prior/concurrent work implements the full composite. Every *component* is anticipated: validate-the-judge norm (Zheng 2023, https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685); validation on the **audited system's own outputs** — Shankar et al. 2024 EvalGen (https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.12272): "We present a mixed-initiative approach to 'validate the validators'—aligning LLM-generated evaluation functions… with human requirements"; formal statistical gate — Calderon et al. 2025 alt-test (https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.10970): "we propose a novel statistical procedure, the Alternative Annotator Test (alt-test), that requires only a modest subset of annotated examples to justify using LLM annotations"; concurrent numeric pre-claim bars — Norman, Rivera & Hughes 2026 MVVP (https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19544): "When test–retest exceeds 0.95, verify position bias is below 0.10 before claiming reliability" (its authors explicitly "do not claim the methodological status of formal pre-registration"); pre-committed thresholds with asymmetric claim margins — Anthropic (https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.21514; Opus 4 system card): "We set a conservative threshold where we can be confident that scores below this threshold rule out 'AI R&D-4'" (asymmetry direction opposite to (d)'s); industry gates (Arize "deploy only after the agreement threshold clears"; Galileo κ ≥ 0.60; FINOS validation on "a representative set of the primary AI's outputs"); generic metrology (attribute MSA κ ≥ 0.75; Krippendorff α ≥ 0.8).
