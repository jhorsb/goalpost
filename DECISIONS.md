**D-012 · Pre-registered extractor-threshold protocol; slice runs both modes (2026-07-05, author instruction at Phase 1 sign-off).**
The extractor self-agreement thresholds (reportability at self-agreement ≥ 0.90; +0.15 margin over measured stability for instability claims and the reason–recourse gap) are design constants chosen by judgement, not derived from data. Protocol: they may be revised **at most once**, **only before any reportable audit is run**, **only on the basis of the Phase 2 slice's calibration data**, with the rationale logged here; any subsequent report that relies on the revised thresholds must disclose both the original and revised values. **D-013 · Environment: Python pinned to 3.12; macOS UF_HIDDEN vs Python 3.14 (2026-07-05).**

**D-027 · Three methodological confounds identified and addressed (2026-07-27, author critique).**
A hostile-read review of write-up v3 identified three soft spots. Verified against the record — two were worse than the critique assumed:
(1) **Granularity asymmetry.** Reasons are counted at four fixed categories, advice at individual recommendations; coarse buckets match more easily, so an unknown share of the 0.535 gap is measurement artifact. **Action:** gap demoted from headline to caveated observation; the **valence-flip result (0.508) promoted to lead finding 3** — it is robust to granularity (given the same topic appears, did its sign change?) and is the dissertation's thesis in sharper form: *explanations stable in what they mention, unstable in what they assert*.
(2) **Extraction rule developed on target data — worse than "model selection".** Not only was extractor calibration run on the target's own transcripts; the **v3 rule itself** ("one entry per category the response uses") was written after observing that this pipeline always emits the same four categories. A rule shaped to a target's structure mechanically flatters that target's topic-stability. The pre-registered *gate* was untouched (and did its job), but the extraction *rule* was not held out. **Action:** disclosed prominently in finding 3 and the limitations box; **protocol change for future audits — extraction rules to be developed on held-out or lab-configuration transcripts, never on the finding-generating data.**
(3) **Lab comparisons architecture-confounded — worse than "different extractor".** All four lab configurations ran **structured** mode (model emits the contract directly, no extraction layer at all); the target ran **freeform + extractor**. Every cross-comparison spanned two measurement architectures. **Action:** stated in "The wider pattern"; plus a **bare-model control** (`phase6/control-bare-model.yaml`, D-028) — same model, corpus, conditions and extraction lens as the target, with a plain screening prompt replacing the 4-agent chain, giving the first apples-to-apples freeform comparison point and isolating pipeline *design* from serving model.
